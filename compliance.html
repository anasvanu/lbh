Supply Chain Policy & Undertaking

LBH Bullion Gold Trading L.L.C • Responsible Precious Metals Sourcing

OECD Annex II Compliant

LBH Bullion Gold Trading L.L.C is committed to conducting all business activities with integrity, transparency, and adherence to the highest ethical and professional standards. We recognize our shared responsibility with all counterparties to maintain a conflict-free, responsible, and transparent supply chain. Our company fully aligns with and commits to the OECD Due Diligence Guidance for Responsible Supply Chains of Minerals from Conflict-Affected and High-Risk Areas, the LBMA Responsible Gold Guidance, the Responsible Minerals Initiative (RMI), the World Gold Council Conflict-Free Gold Standard, and all applicable UAE Ministry of Economy (MOEC) legislations and regulatory requirements.

Our Core Commitments

Zero Tolerance for Human Rights Violations

We neither participate in nor benefit from any form of human rights abuse, including inhumane treatment, forced labor, child labor, human trafficking, or criminal activity. Engagement is immediately suspended if any risk is identified.

No Support to Non-State Armed Groups

Strict prohibition of direct or indirect procurement from areas controlled by non-state armed groups, illegal taxation at mine sites or trade routes, or extortion points across the supply chain.

Anti-Bribery and Anti-Corruption

We do not offer, promise, give, solicit, or accept bribes or improper advantages, resisting any attempt to misrepresent the origin of precious metals, assay values, or government fees.

AML & Counter-Terrorist Financing

Implementing a robust compliance framework aligned with international FATF standards, screening against global sanctions lists, and reporting suspicious activity to the UAE Financial Intelligence Unit.

Due Diligence & Risk Management: Comprehensive, risk-based due diligence is conducted prior to establishing business relationships, with continuous transaction monitoring aligned with OECD Annex II five-step framework.

Record Retention: Complete, accurate, and auditable records of all precious metals counterparties and transactions are maintained for the statutory period mandated by UAE law.

Staff Training & Continuous Education: Regular training workshops ensure deal officers and compliance personnel maintain full mastery over evolving regulatory standards.

Due Diligence Protocol

Precious Metals Due Diligence Questionnaire

Review the compliance modules examined during counterparty verification.

1 Company & Regulated Entity Details

Legal name of regulated entity, registered office address, date & country of incorporation, Commercial Registration No., Corporate Tax No., VAT No., direct and indirect subsidiaries (with complete organization chart), designated contact officer, and declaration regarding any legal proceedings or regulatory investigations within the past 5 years.

2 Business Activity & Sourcing Profile

Classification of counterparty (Bank, Jeweller, Precious Metals Trader/Dealer, Refinery, Wholesaler, Mint, Financial Intermediary). Identification of core business activities, specific trade licenses, primary market presence (UAE, GCC, International), and exact precious metal product lines handled.

3 Ultimate Beneficial Ownership (UBO > 25%) & Governance

Full disclosure of all natural persons holding 25% or greater equity or voting rights. Full names, residential addresses, nationality, dates of birth, and Politically Exposed Person (PEP) status. Management hierarchy disclosure including Board of Directors, Senior Dealing Officers, and the designated Compliance Officer.

4 Origin of Physical Precious Metals & CAHRA Verification

Complete origin transparency: full list of country origins, verification whether metals originate from or transit through Conflict-Affected and High-Risk Areas (CAHRA as defined on the EU CAHRA list), import license validation, refining and melting facilities verification, and percentage breakdown of recycled precious metals versus primary mined material (LSM / ASM).

5 AML/CFT Policies, Shell Bank Prohibition & Anti-Bribery

Confirmation of written AML/CFT policies approved by Senior Management, designated Compliance Officer oversight, independent compliance audit history, strict prohibition of accounts or dealings with shell banks, comprehensive PEP relationship policies, and anti-bribery declarations.

6 Transaction Monitoring & Settlement Channels

Analysis of normal versus unusual transaction patterns, payment method splits (Bank Wire %, Cash %, Metal-to-Metal settlement %), strict cash limits compliance, anti-smurfing procedures, secure logistics/transport methods (Road, Air, Armored Transport), and formal Suspicious Transaction Reporting (STR) protocols.
